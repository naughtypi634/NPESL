At the airport

机场

At the check-in counter在值机柜台

对方可能这样问

要护照

句型Can I see…? / May I have…? / Could you hand me…? / …, please.

Can I 常读成「k'n I」，May I 会连读。

问去哪

句型Where are you flying / headed? / What's your (final) destination?

问行李

句型Are you checking any bags? / Do you have any bags to check? / How many bags…?

问座位

句型Do you have a preference? / Window or aisle? / Any preference for…?

At the bag drop在行李托运柜台

对方可能这样问

让称重

句型Place / put … on the scale. / Go ahead and weigh it.

问包内物品

句型Any liquids / batteries / sharp objects…? / Is there anything…?

说超重

句型It's … over. / You're … over the limit. / This one's overweight.

问直挂

句型Is this going all the way to…? / Are you checking it through to…?

through / straight through 都是「直挂」。

拿电脑

句型Take … out of… / Put … in a separate tray.

查液体

At the information desk在问询台

对方可能这样问

主动帮忙

句型Can I help you…? / What can I do for you? / Are you looking for…?

问去哪个口

句型Which … are you looking for? / Where are you trying to…? / Do you know…?

问转机

句型Are you connecting…? / What's your connecting flight? / Did you miss…?

说延误/改签

句型… has been delayed. / The next flight is… / Would you like me to rebook you?

flight's = flight has，听上去像「flight's」。
